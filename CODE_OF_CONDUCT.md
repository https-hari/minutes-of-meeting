# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project (in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews) should be able
to do so without harassment, whatever their background, identity, experience level or the tools they use.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and kind.
- Accept constructive criticism, and credit other people's ideas.
- Help newcomers: link to docs, explain decisions, keep jargon down.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment in public or private, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including content from their chat logs, without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or trolling.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, Hari Sharma ([@https-hari](https://github.com/https-hari)),
through the contact details on the GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
Please include links or screenshots where you can.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may, depending on severity:

1. **Correct**: a private note explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warn**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.
